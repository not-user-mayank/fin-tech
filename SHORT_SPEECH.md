=== 30-SECOND SPEECH (Very Short & Simple) ===

We split the work. I built the dashboard, my partner built the brain. Python only.

The app reads real transaction data and gives every merchant a credit score from 300 to 900 — like a CIBIL score. It shows risk band, loan limit, and exactly why the score is what it is. New merchants get a fair start.

The anomaly detector finds suspicious spending peaks, so banks catch fraud fast.

Banks make safer loans. Merchants know how to improve. One command to deploy. Done.
